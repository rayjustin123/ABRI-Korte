              How booking works

              Booking takes a minute.

              1. Pick a time

                Our schedule shows every open hour, with the price worked out for you.

              2. Send it on Messenger

                Tap Book and your date, time and hours are written into the message for you.

              3. Lock it in

                Pay one hour as a reservation fee by GCash or cash. Your court is held once we confirm, and you pay the rest when you arrive.
